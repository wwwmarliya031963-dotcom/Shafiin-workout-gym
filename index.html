NUTRITION GUIDE

EAT FOR YOUR GOAL

Build meals around protein, vegetables, whole-food carbohydrates and healthy fats.

✓

EAT MORE OFTEN

  • Eggs, chicken, fish and lean meats
  • Dal, beans, chickpeas and tofu
  • Rice, oats, potatoes and whole grains
  • Vegetables and seasonal fruit
  • Nuts, seeds and healthy oils
  • Water and unsweetened drinks
!

LIMIT

  • Deep-fried and heavily processed foods
  • Sugary drinks and excess sweets
  • Frequent fast food and oversized portions
  • Very salty packaged snacks
  • Alcohol, if you drink
  • Extreme diets or crash fasting

WEEKLY PROGRAMS

YOUR ROUTINE

Choose a routine that matches your experience and recovery capacity.

BEGINNER

3-DAY FULL BODY

Great starting point for building consistency.

  1. MON Full Body A — Squat, bench, row, core
  2. WED Full Body B — Deadlift, press, pulldown, legs
  3. FRI Full Body A — Squat, bench, row, core
INTERMEDIATE

4-DAY UPPER / LOWER

Balanced strength and muscle-building schedule.

  1. MON Upper — Chest, back, shoulders, arms
  2. TUE Lower — Quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves
  3. THU Upper — Chest, back, shoulders, arms
  4. FRI Lower — Legs + core
FAT LOSS

5-DAY ACTIVE

Strength training plus manageable cardio.

  1. MON Push + 15 min cardio
  2. TUE Pull + 15 min cardio
  3. WED Walk + mobility
  4. THU Legs + core
  5. FRI Full body + cardio
